Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

OurMynd ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the OurMynd mobile application and related services (the "App").

OurMynd® is based in the United Kingdom.

By using OurMynd, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Who We Are

OurMynd is an AI-powered coaching and productivity application designed to support people with ADHD, executive dysfunction, and related challenges. We are the "data controller" responsible for your personal data under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Contact: support@ourmynd.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Information you provide directly

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password (or Apple/Google sign-in identifier)
  • Onboarding information: details you share during setup, including your goals, challenges, preferred routines, energy patterns, and how you like to be supported
  • Conversations: the messages you send to and receive from the AI coach
  • Tasks and goals: the tasks, goals, habits, and reflections you create in the App
  • Payment information: processed securely by Stripe (we do not store your full card details)

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: how you interact with the App, features used, and session information
  • Device information: device type, operating system, and app version
  • Notification preferences: your chosen reminder channels and times

Sensitive information

Because OurMynd supports people with ADHD and related conditions, some information you share may relate to your mental health or wellbeing. We treat this as special category data under UK GDPR and apply additional safeguards. We only process this information with your explicit consent, which you provide by choosing to use the App and share this information with your AI coach.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, personalise, and improve the AI coaching experience
  • Generate your daily plans, reminders, and reflections
  • Remember your goals, preferences, and patterns over time so your coach becomes more helpful
  • Send you notifications and reminders via your chosen channels (push, email, SMS, or WhatsApp)
  • Process your subscription payments
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the App
  • Comply with our legal obligations

4. AI Processing and Third-Party AI Providers

OurMynd uses artificial intelligence to power your coaching experience. To do this, your conversations and relevant context are sent securely to trusted third-party AI providers, including Anthropic (Claude) and OpenAI, who process this data to generate responses.

  • These providers process your data only to return a response to the App
  • We do not permit these providers to use your conversations to train their models where an option to opt out is available to us
  • Your data is transmitted using encryption

You should avoid sharing information you would not want processed by a third-party AI system. OurMynd is a coaching and productivity tool and is not a medical device, therapist, or crisis service. Please see our Terms & Conditions.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Under UK GDPR, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent: for processing special category (health-related) data and for marketing communications
  • Contract: to provide the services you sign up for
  • Legitimate interests: to improve and secure our services, where these interests are not overridden by your rights
  • Legal obligation: where we are required to process data by law

You may withdraw consent at any time (see Your Rights below).

6. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI) to generate coaching responses
  • Payment processors (Stripe) to handle subscriptions
  • Infrastructure providers (e.g. cloud hosting, database, and notification services such as Supabase, Firebase, Twilio) to operate the App
  • Legal authorities where required by law

All third parties are bound by data protection obligations and may only use your data as instructed by us.

7. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers are based outside the UK, including in the United States. Where we transfer your data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms approved under UK GDPR.

8. Data Retention

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain certain information for legal, accounting, or fraud-prevention purposes.

9. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit and access controls. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Data portability: receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@ourmynd.com. You can also delete your account and data directly within the App.

11. Children

OurMynd is not intended for children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the App or email. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised.
