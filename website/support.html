Support

Something not working, or something you need us to look at? Here is how to reach a person.

Email us

support@ourmynd.com

We aim to reply within two working days. Reports of abuse or harmful content are looked at first.

Reporting something in the app

You can report any post, message, room or person from inside OurMynd, and we review every report.

  • In a focus room - press and hold someone's avatar.
  • Body doubling - tap "Report or block" under your partner's name.
  • In a conversation - tap the … button at the top right.

If it is urgent, or you would rather not use the app, email us at the address above with as much detail as you can.

Blocking someone

Blocking is in the same place as reporting, and takes effect immediately. Once you block someone you stop seeing each other, and they cannot send you a request or start a conversation with you.

You can see who you have blocked, and undo it, in Profile › Blocked accounts.

Deleting your account

Go to Profile › Account and choose delete my account. You will be asked to type a confirmation. This removes your account and the data attached to it.

You can also export your data from the same screen, or email us and we will do it for you.

Your mood and energy data

OurMynd asks separately, on its own screen, before it processes anything health-related. Declining does not limit the rest of the app, and you can withdraw that consent at any time in Settings.

What OurMynd is not

OurMynd is a planning and focus tool. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose or treat any condition, and nothing in it is a substitute for professional advice.

If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis line in your country. In the UK you can call Samaritans free on 116 123, at any hour.
