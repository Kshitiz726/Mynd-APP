3Voice dumptap the orb

Talk as long as you like. Nothing interrupts you.

Most voice features cut you off the moment you pause to think, which for an ADHD brain is exactly the wrong moment. This one records straight through until you tap done. Then the whole recording is transcribed at once and run through the same breakdown the typed dump uses.

No pause detection, no silence timeout, no lost sentence
The orb swells with your voice, so you can see it listening
Ends in the same place as typing: real tasks in the backlog
