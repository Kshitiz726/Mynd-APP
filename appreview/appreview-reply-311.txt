Hello,

Thank you for the review. We would like to provide detail on the 3.1.1 point,
because we believe the build under review does not contain a purchase path on
iOS, and we want to make sure we fix the right thing.

Build 19 cannot sell anything on iOS. The app has a single switch that decides
whether a build is allowed to sell, in lib/core/config/store_policy.dart, and it
returns false on iOS. Everything that could lead to a purchase is behind it:

1. The paywall screen is not registered as a route on iOS, so there is no way to
   reach it, including by deep link.
2. The checkout call and the billing portal call both return immediately on iOS
   without opening anything.
3. No upgrade button is rendered anywhere in the app on iOS.
4. The plan card in Settings is not tappable on iOS.
5. The message quota text on iOS does not mention Plus, Pro or any price.

Rather than show an iOS user a paywall they could not complete, this build gives
the paid features away at no charge on iOS. There is no digital content in the
iOS build that is withheld pending payment, and no mechanism in the iOS build by
which a payment can be made.

We have also confirmed there is no purchase page reachable from the links inside
the app. The only external links are our terms, privacy and copyright pages, and
our website has no pricing, plans, upgrade or subscribe page.

We are not disputing the finding so much as asking for help reproducing it. If a
purchase path was reached on the iPad Air 11-inch (M3), could you tell us which
screen it was reached from, and what it displayed? We will fix it immediately.

If the finding refers to subscriptions being available on our website or on
Android, those are separate storefronts and are not offered or referenced inside
the iOS app.

We are separately planning In-App Purchase so that iOS users can buy Plus and
Pro through StoreKit rather than receiving them at no charge, and we will submit
those products for review when they are ready.

Thank you for your time.
