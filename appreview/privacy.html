Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the OurMynd mobile application and related services (the "App"), and what rights you have over that information.

1. Who We Are

OurMynd is provided by The Social Globe Ltd ("we", "us", "our"), a company registered in England and Wales under company number 13923693, whose registered office is at 2nd Floor, 112 High Road, Ilford, England, IG1 1BY. OurMynd® is a registered trade mark of The Social Globe Ltd.

We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy, under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and, where you are in the European Economic Area, the EU General Data Protection Regulation.

Contact: support@ourmynd.com

OurMynd is an AI-powered coaching and productivity application designed to support people with ADHD, executive dysfunction, and related challenges.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password, or your Apple or Google sign-in identifier
  • Age confirmation: confirmation that you are 16 or over, and your date of birth where you provide it
  • Onboarding information: details you share during setup, including your goals, challenges, preferred routines, energy patterns, and how you like to be supported
  • Conversations: the messages you send to and receive from the AI coach
  • Voice notes: if you record a voice note, the audio is uploaded and converted to text so the coach can read it. We keep the resulting text as part of your conversation. The audio itself is not retained after transcription
  • Tasks and goals: the tasks, goals, habits, streaks, and reflections you create in the App
  • Shared sessions: which focus rooms and body doubling sessions you join, when, and the generated display name shown to the other participants. Audio in a body doubling session is transmitted live between participants and is not recorded or stored by us. We also keep any report you make about another user

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: how you interact with the App, features used, and session information
  • Device information: device type, operating system, and app version
  • Notification preferences: your chosen reminder times and whether push notifications are enabled

Payment information

We do not currently take payment through the App, and we do not collect or store any card details. Every feature is available at no charge while our subscription plans are being finalised.

When paid plans launch, subscriptions will be sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase. Apple takes the payment and holds the card details; we never receive or store your card number. We receive only confirmation that a subscription is active and which plan it is. Apple’s handling of your payment is governed by Apple’s own privacy policy. We will update this policy before paid plans go live.

If you subscribed through our website before paid plans were withdrawn, we hold a record of that payment. See section 8 for how long we keep it.

Sensitive information

Because OurMynd supports people with ADHD and related conditions, some of what you share may reveal information about your mental health or wellbeing. This is special category data under data protection law and we apply additional safeguards to it.

We process it only where you have given us your explicit consent. That consent is asked for separately in the App, on its own screen, before you share anything of this kind. It is not bundled into your acceptance of our Terms & Conditions, it is not a condition of creating an account, and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings without losing access to the rest of the App. Withdrawing it stops future processing and lets you delete what has already been stored.

4. AI Processing and Third-Party AI Providers

The coach does not run on your phone. To write a reply, what you send has to be transmitted to an AI provider outside OurMynd. This section sets out exactly what is sent, who it is sent to, and what they may do with it.

We ask before any of it happens. The first time you open the coach, the App shows you a disclosure naming the provider and asks you to agree. Nothing is sent to any AI provider until you do. You can withdraw at any time in Settings, after which the coach stops and nothing further is sent. Declining affects the coach only; the rest of the App is unchanged.

What is sent

  • The messages you type to the coach
  • The context the coach is given to answer with: the relevant parts of your goals, tasks, routines and saved memory
  • Your mood and memory entries, only if you have separately agreed to that. Health-related context is governed by its own explicit consent, asked separately (see section 2). If you decline it, the coach still works and that context is not included
  • Voice-note audio, if you record one. Voice notes are uploaded and converted to text so that the coach can read them

We do not send your name, your email address, your payment details, or your location to any AI provider.

Who it is sent to

  • Groq, Inc. (United States) is the default provider, and handles both coaching replies and voice-note transcription. Unless you have connected your own account with another provider, Groq is the only AI provider that receives your data
  • If you choose to connect your own AI account, the App can instead route your messages through Puter, which passes them to the model you select: Anthropic (Claude models) or Google (Gemini models). This is off by default. It happens only if you connect an account yourself, and the App names the provider on screen and asks for your agreement again before switching, because agreeing to one recipient is not agreeing to another
  • We do not send your data to OpenAI

What they may do with it

  • These providers act as our processors. They may use your data only to produce the response they return to us, and only on our instructions. They may not use it for their own purposes
  • Your conversations are not used to train AI models. Our agreements with these providers prohibit the use of data submitted through their business interfaces to train or improve their models
  • Your data is encrypted in transit. Providers may retain content briefly for abuse monitoring where their own legal obligations require it, and then delete it
  • These providers are in the United States. Transfers are made under the safeguards set out in section 7
  • If we change AI provider, we will update this policy before the change takes effect, and the App will ask for your agreement again before anything is sent to the new one

OurMynd is a coaching and productivity tool and is not a medical device, therapist, or crisis service. Please see our Terms & Conditions.

5. Personalisation and Profiling

The App builds a picture of you over time, drawn from your goals, tasks, routines, reported energy patterns, and conversations, so that the plans and prompts it produces fit you better. This is profiling as data protection law defines it.

  • It is used only to personalise the coaching, planning, and reminders you receive
  • It is not used to make any decision that produces legal effects concerning you or that similarly significantly affects you, so Article 22 does not apply
  • It is not used to build advertising audiences, and we do not share it with advertisers
  • Users under 18 are not profiled for marketing purposes at all
  • You can see what the App has remembered about you, edit it, and delete it, from the Memory section of the App

6. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data, and we never have. We share it only with the following categories of recipient:

  • AI providers to generate coaching responses and transcribe voice notes: Groq by default, and Puter with Anthropic or Google if you connect your own AI account. See section 4
  • Payment processing: none at present, because the App does not take payment. When paid plans launch, Apple will process the payment through In-App Purchase. See section 2
  • Hosting, database and authentication (Supabase, Render, Google, Apple) to run the App and sign you in
  • Real-time audio and video (LiveKit) where you join a body doubling or focus room
  • Other users, who see your generated display name and hear your live audio while you are in a focus room or a body doubling session with them. They are never shown your real name or email address
  • Professional advisers such as accountants and lawyers, where necessary
  • Legal authorities, where we are required to disclose by law, or where disclosure is necessary to protect someone from serious harm
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is sold or reorganised. You will be told before your data is transferred, and this policy will continue to apply to it

Every processor is bound by a written contract that requires them to protect your data, to use it only as we instruct, and to delete or return it when the work ends.

7. International Data Transfers

Some of our providers are based outside the UK and the EEA, including in the United States. Where your data is transferred there, we rely on:

  • The UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses, for transfers from the UK
  • The European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses, for transfers from the EEA
  • Adequacy regulations where the destination country has been recognised as providing adequate protection, including the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified under it

We assess the risk of each transfer and apply additional safeguards, including encryption in transit and at rest, where they are needed. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards that apply to a particular transfer by emailing support@ourmynd.com.

9. How We Protect Your Data

  • All data is encrypted in transit using TLS, and encrypted at rest by our hosting and database providers
  • Access to production systems is restricted to the people who need it, protected by multi-factor authentication, and logged
  • Credentials and API keys are held in a secrets store, never in our source code
  • We apply security updates to our dependencies and infrastructure on an ongoing basis
  • We keep the amount of personal data we hold to what we actually need

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. If Something Goes Wrong

If a personal data breach occurs, we will report it to the Information Commissioner’s Office within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, unless it is unlikely to result in a risk to you. Where a breach is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will tell you directly and without undue delay, and explain what has happened and what you can do.

11. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Be informed about how we use your data, which is what this policy is for
  • Access the personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase your data, often called the right to be forgotten
  • Restrict our processing while a dispute about it is resolved
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to object to direct marketing at any time, which we will always honour
  • Portability, meaning you can receive the data you gave us in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, and ask us to send it to another provider
  • Withdraw consent at any time, including your consent to our processing of health-related information, without affecting anything done before you withdrew it
  • Not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We do not make such decisions

To exercise any right, email support@ourmynd.com. We respond within one month, and will tell you if we need longer because a request is complex. Exercising your rights is free, and we will not treat you differently for doing so. You can also export or delete your account and data directly within the App.

Complaints. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority:

  • In the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk, or 0303 123 1113
  • In the EEA, the data protection authority in the country where you live, work, or where the problem occurred

12. Users Aged 16 and 17, and Children

OurMynd is not for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16, and we ask you to confirm your age when you sign up. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 16, we close it and delete the data. If you believe a child under 16 has given us personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

For users who are 16 or 17, we follow the Information Commissioner’s Age Appropriate Design Code. In practice that means:

  • Privacy settings are set to the most protective option by default
  • They are not matched with adults through automatic body doubling matchmaking
  • Profiles are private by default
  • We do not profile them for marketing, and we do not send them marketing
  • We do not use design techniques intended to encourage them to give us more data or stay in the App longer than they intend
  • We collect the minimum data needed for the features they actually use

The OurMynd website sets no advertising or tracking cookies, and no analytics cookies. Nothing you do here is used to build a profile of you. The only information processed is what your browser sends in the ordinary course of loading the pages, which our hosting provider records for security and reliability.
