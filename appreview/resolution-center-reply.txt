2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
A planning app for adults with ADHD and anyone who finds ordinary to-do apps
hard to keep up with. A blank task list asks you to be organised before it is
any use, which is the one thing our users cannot do on demand. Here you empty
your head in one messy sentence and the app splits that into sized tasks and
schedules them around your day. Not a medical device; no diagnostic or
treatment claims.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS
No credentials exist to give: there is no username and password path. Sign in
with Apple or Google creates the account on the first tap, with no password
and no email confirmation, so no demo account can exist. Please use "Continue
with Apple" with your own Apple ID. One account type, no admin tier.
Chat, where the app opens, is the coach. Brain Dump: tap (+) in the tab bar,
choose it, type several mixed-up tasks in one sentence, then "Process with AI";
they appear on Schedule. Focus: pick a length and start a timer. Settings and
account are behind the drawer button at the top left of Chat, then the gear.
The Notes field has the full walkthrough.
Matchmaking is the only route into a shared room: no room browser, no
join-by-code, so a reviewer alone will not be paired. To see it live, reply
with a date and time and we will have a person waiting.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Groq: AI inference for brain dump parsing, coach replies and Whisper
transcription, via our own server, which authenticates every request; the app
holds no AI key. Puter.js: an in-app WebView that
records the mic and speaks replies aloud. Sign in with Apple and Google
Sign-In: authentication. Render: hosting for our API server. Supabase
PostgreSQL: our database. YouTube IFrame Player: music a user pastes into a
focus room. Gmail IMAP: optional, only if a user connects their own mailbox.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app functions consistently in all regions. English only, nothing
region-locked or gated by territory.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
We are not a healthcare provider and are not regulated as one. No diagnosis,
treatment or clinical advice, and the coach directs anyone describing a crisis
to emergency services. The onboarding health question is optional consent for
using wellbeing context in coaching. The only
third-party content is music a user chooses by pasting a YouTube link, played
through YouTube's official player; the Feed briefing is generated text that
links out to publishers.
Paid content: this version sells nothing on iOS. No In-App Purchases, no
paywall, no external payment link, so there is no purchase flow.
