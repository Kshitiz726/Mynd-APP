### Where you bought it decides who refunds you

This matters, because we cannot refund a payment we never received.

**Bought inside the iPhone or iPad app.** Apple takes the payment, not us.
Refunds are handled by Apple and are requested at
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). We cannot issue,
approve or speed up an Apple refund, and we are not told the outcome. Manage or
cancel the subscription in Settings on your device, under your Apple Account,
then Subscriptions.

**Bought on our website, or anywhere other than the App Store.** We take the
payment through Stripe, and the rest of this policy applies.

### Your 14 day right to cancel

If you are a consumer in the UK or the EU, you have a legal right to cancel a
purchase within 14 days of buying it, without giving a reason, under the
Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges)
Regulations 2013.

There is one exception you should know about, because it is easy to trigger
without noticing. A subscription gives you the paid features immediately. If
you ask for that immediate access and acknowledge that you lose the 14 day right
once the service has been fully supplied, the right ends at that point. In
practice we do not hold people to this: see below.

### What we actually do

We would rather someone left on good terms than stayed on a plan they regret.

- **Within 14 days of your first payment**, tell us and we will refund it in
  full, whether or not you have used the paid features, and whether or not the
  strict legal right still applies.
- **A renewal you did not expect.** If a monthly or yearly plan renewed and you
  tell us within 14 days of that charge, we refund it in full, provided you have
  not made significant use of the paid features in that new period.
- **Outside those windows**, we do not generally refund part-used time. You can
  cancel at any time and you keep the paid features until the end of the period
  you have already paid for.
- **If OurMynd did not work**, and the problem was ours rather than your device
  or connection, we will refund you regardless of timing. Tell us what happened
  and we will look at it properly.

### Cancelling

Cancel at any time from Settings in the app, or through the billing portal
linked from the paywall. Cancelling stops the next payment. It does not delete
your account, and it does not delete your data.

Deleting your account is separate and is also in Settings. Deleting the account
does not automatically refund an active subscription, so cancel or ask for a
refund first if you want both.

Consumer Rights Act 2015 if the service is not as described, not of
