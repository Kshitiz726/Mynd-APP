Thank you for the detailed report. The review date, device and OS version you
included let us identify the cause precisely.

What caused it

The failure was in our backend service, not in the submitted binary.

On October 30, the date of your review, our hosting provider suspended our API
host after the account exceeded a resource limit on its plan. While suspended,
the host returned HTTP 503 Service Unavailable to every request.

Sign in with Apple, Sign in with Google and guest sign in all authenticate
against that same API, which is why all three failed together with an identical
error rather than failing individually. The app was correctly surfacing the
server error it received.

What we have fixed

The API host is restored and serving normally. We have also moved the backend
to a dedicated paid hosting plan, so the resource limit that caused the
suspension no longer applies and the service runs continuously.

What we verified on iPad

We reproduced your review environment rather than testing only on our own
devices. On an iPad Air 11-inch, the same device family used in your review, we
installed version 1.0 (19) and confirmed that the app launches, the sign in
screen renders correctly, guest sign in completes, and the authenticated
screens load live data from our API without errors.

The app is built for iPhone and so runs on iPad in iPhone compatibility mode.
That is the configuration your reviewer used, and it now behaves exactly as it
does on iPhone.

What we verified on iPhone

On the identical build now in review, all three sign in paths complete
successfully and reach the authenticated app: Continue with Apple, Continue
with Google, and Continue as guest. We also confirmed the backend is returning
healthy responses and that our payment configuration is intact and unchanged.

Signing in without an account

The first screen offers a "Continue as guest" button. One tap creates a session
and opens the full app with every feature available. No account, credentials,
email address or demo data are needed at any point, on either device.

No new binary is required

The defect was entirely server side and has been resolved server side. The
build you already have is correct and unmodified, so we respectfully ask that
you continue your review of it rather than requiring a new submission.

We are glad to provide any further detail that would help.
