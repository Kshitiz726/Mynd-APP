1. ACCESSING THE APP - TAP "CONTINUE AS GUEST"
Build 19 adds a third button to the login screen: "Continue as guest". One tap
creates a fresh, empty account and opens the app, with no password, no email
and no provider. That is the demo access you asked for, added for this
review. "Continue with Apple" and "Continue with Google" behave
identically; there is no username and password path anywhere, which is why no
credentials exist to type. One account type, no admin tier. The recording was
captured on build 18, before this button existed.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
A planning app for adults with ADHD and anyone who finds normal to-do apps
hard to use. A blank task list asks you to be organised first; here you empty
your head in one messy sentence and the app turns that into sized tasks
scheduled around your day. Not a medical device, no diagnostic claims.

3. REACHING EACH FEATURE
Four tabs (Chat, Feed, Schedule, Focus) and a (+) in the centre.
- AI coach: the Chat tab, where the app opens. Type, or tap a prompt such as
  "i'm stuck". It answers using the tasks the app can see.
- Brain Dump: tap (+), choose it, type several mixed-up tasks in one sentence,
  then "Process with AI". They land on Schedule, with the week strip, goals
  and Auto-Schedule.
- Feed tab: an AI briefing, the next task, a mood check-in.
- Focus tab: pick a length and start a solo timer.
- Settings and account: Chat tab, drawer button top left, then the gear.

4. USER CONTENT, REPORTING AND BLOCKING (Guideline 1.2)
The only user-to-user feature is body doubling: two people focusing in a
shared room. No public feed, no browsable profiles, no direct messages, and
another person sees a random display name, not your real name.
- Focus tab, turn on "body doubling", start a session, tap "Find a partner".
  Matchmaking pairs you with another waiting person.
- The moment you are paired, "Report or block" sits under the partner's name,
  before you enter the room.
- Inside the room, long-press the other participant's tile to report or block.
- Blocked people are listed, and unblockable, at Profile > Blocked accounts.
- Matchmaking is the only way into a room, so a reviewer alone will not be
  paired, which is why it is filmed on two devices. To test it live, reply
  with a time and we will have someone waiting.

5. ACCOUNT DELETION (Guideline 5.1.1(v))
Profile > "Account and your data" > "delete my account", with a typed
confirmation. Deletes the account and its data, server side and on device.

6. PAID CONTENT
None on iOS. This version sells nothing, has no In-App Purchases, and unlocks
every feature for everyone. No paywall, no external payment link.

7. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Groq: brain dump to tasks, coach replies, voice transcription (Whisper), via
  our own server, which authenticates every request.
- Puter.js: in-app WebView, records the mic and speaks replies aloud.
- Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In: authentication.
- Render (hosting) and Supabase PostgreSQL (database), operated by us.
- YouTube IFrame Player: music pasted into a focus room.
- Gmail IMAP: optional, if a user connects their own mailbox.

8. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Identical everywhere, English only, nothing gated by region.

9. REGULATED INDUSTRY AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not a healthcare provider and not regulated as one. No diagnosis, treatment or
clinical advice, and the coach directs anyone in crisis to emergency services.
The onboarding health question is optional consent for wellbeing context. Third-party material is limited to music a user supplies as a
YouTube link, played through YouTube's official player.
