# OurMynd: App Review screen recording, build 18

Replaces the September brief, which was written against build 10 and a Home tab
that no longer exists. Apple's request of 15 September sets the order: launch
first, then registration, login and deletion, then user content with reporting
and blocking, then paid content.

Target 4 to 6 minutes. Nothing edited, nothing sped up.

## Before you record

- A physical iPhone on the latest iOS. A simulator capture is rejected.
- Build 18 from TestFlight on both phones.
- **Two phones and two Apple IDs.** Step 6 cannot be filmed alone: matchmaking
  is the only route into a shared room and it will not pair you with yourself.
- Signed out of OurMynd on the recording phone, or reinstall it. Apple asked to
  see registration.
- Do Not Disturb on, battery above 50 percent, brightness up.
- Start on the phone's home screen with the app closed.
- Use the spare Apple ID on the recording phone if you do not want step 9 to
  delete your own account, because it deletes for real.

## 1. Launch, 15 sec

Tap the OurMynd icon from the home screen. Let the splash finish and the login
screen appear on its own. Do not cut this: Apple asked for the recording to
begin with the app being launched.

## 2. Registration, 30 sec

Tap **Continue with Apple** and complete the Apple sheet. Land on the Chat tab.
Dismiss the notification prompt with "not now".

This is the shot that explains why there is no demo account: one tap, no
password, no email confirmation.

## 3. Brain Dump, 60 sec

Tap **(+)** in the centre of the bottom bar, choose **Brain Dump**. Type one
messy sentence containing four different tasks. Tap **Process with AI** and wait
without tapping again.

Then open the **Schedule** tab and show the four tasks sitting under
**backlog** as separate items. This is the core value of the app and it should be the clearest thing in
the video.

## 4. AI coach, 45 sec

Back to the **Chat** tab. Show that it opens straight into the conversation with
no second sign-up. Tap a prompt such as **i'm stuck**, wait for the whole reply,
and leave it on screen long enough to read. The reply refers to the tasks from
step 3; let that land.

## 5. Focus, 20 sec

Open the **Focus** tab, pick a length such as 25, start the timer, let it run a
few seconds, then stop it.

## 6. Reporting and blocking, 90 sec, two phones

The most important segment. This is the Guideline 1.2 evidence.

On the recording phone: **Focus** tab, turn **body doubling** on, start the
session, then tap **Find a partner**. The second person does the same at the
same moment on the other phone.

Once it says you are paired, film in this order:
1. **Report or block** under the partner's name, before entering the room. Open
   it, show the options, come out again.
2. Tap **Start focusing together** to enter the shared room.
3. **Long-press the other participant's tile.** Show the report and block sheet.
4. Submit a block.
5. Leave the room. Open the drawer from the Chat tab, tap the gear, then
   **Blocked accounts**, and show the blocked person listed with the option to
   unblock.

Say nothing on camera about there being no other route in; that goes in the
written reply.

## 7. Support contact, 10 sec

Still in Profile, tap **Contact support** so support@ourmynd.com is visible.

## 8. Login, 25 sec

Profile, scroll to **Logout**, confirm. The login screen returns. Tap
**Continue with Apple** again and show it landing on the same account with the
data still there.

Apple asked for registration *and* login as separate flows. This is the login
one.

## 9. Account deletion, 40 sec

Profile, **Account and your data**, then **delete my account**. Show the typed
confirmation, complete it, and keep recording until the login screen comes back.

Then sign in once more with the same Apple ID and show the app comes up empty,
with no tasks and no conversation. That is the proof the deletion was real, and
it is worth the extra 20 seconds because it is the thing a reviewer cannot
verify any other way.

## Do not show

- Any paywall, price or payment. This build sells nothing on iOS, which is the
  answer to Apple's item about paid content.
- A Puter sign-in screen. If one appears, the phone is on an old build. Stop.
- Edited or sped-up waiting. Real loading times are expected.
