Our published legal documents at ourmynd.com/privacy, /terms and /copyright
now describe three protections the app does not yet implement. The documents
are live, so the app is currently out of step with them. Build these three,
matching the existing app conventions (OmTappable, OmScrollBehavior, Lucide
icons, no default Material widgets, iOS-smooth feel).

1. SEPARATE CONSENT FOR HEALTH-RELATED DATA

The privacy policy states that consent to process health-related information
is asked for on its own screen, is not bundled into acceptance of the Terms,
is not a condition of creating an account, and can be withdrawn in Settings
at any time without losing access to the rest of the app.

Build:
- A dedicated onboarding screen, after account creation and before any coach
  conversation, that explains what will be processed and why, and takes an
  explicit opt-in. It must be a deliberate action, not a pre-ticked box, and
  the user must be able to decline and still use the app.
- Persist the decision server-side with a timestamp and the policy version,
  not only in SharedPreferences, because it has to be evidenced later. Add a
  migration for it alongside the existing ones in server/.
- A Settings entry showing the current state, the date it was given, and a
  withdraw action. Withdrawing must stop future processing and offer to
  delete what has already been stored.
- Gate the coach and the memory system on this consent. If it is absent or
  withdrawn, the coach must not receive or store health-related context.

2. REPORT AND BLOCK FOR THE COMMUNITY FEATURES

Terms section 6 states that reporting tools exist in the app, that reports
are reviewed within 24 hours, that users can block each other, and that
repeat offenders are terminated. Apple Guideline 1.2 requires all of this
before a user-generated-content app is approved.

The community surfaces are server/src/routes/rooms.ts, social.ts, feed.ts,
bodydoubling.ts and chat.ts. There is no moderation route today.

Build:
- A moderation route in server/src/routes with tables for reports, blocks,
  and moderation actions, plus migrations.
- Report on every surface where one user can see another user's content or
  presence: feed posts, chat messages, room participants, profiles. Reason
  picker plus optional free text.
- Block a user: they can no longer message the reporter, see their content,
  or appear to them. Enforce it server-side in every query, not in the UI.
- Hide reported content from the reporter immediately, before review.
- A strike count per account and automatic suspension at the threshold.
- An admin view or CLI to read the queue and act on it, since the 24 hour
  commitment needs somebody able to see the reports.

3. AGE DECLARATION AND UNDER-18 DEFAULTS

The documents state a minimum age of 16, that age is confirmed at sign-up,
that accounts found to be under 16 are suspended and deleted, and that 16
and 17 year olds get protective defaults under the ICO Age Appropriate
Design Code.

Build:
- Date of birth at sign-up, stored server-side. Under 16 is refused, and the
  refusal must not be trivially retried by going back and changing the date.
- A derived is_minor flag on the account for under-18s.
- For minors: profile private by default, not surfaced in suggestions to
  unconnected adults, direct messages from unknown users off by default, and
  no marketing or marketing profiling.
- Enforce every one of these server-side.
